JARVIS ULTIMATE

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JARVIS Ultimate ("JARVIS", "we", "our" or "the bot") may collect, use, store and protect information when it is used in Discord servers. Please read this policy before using the bot.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and how the bot is configured, JARVIS may process the following information:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs and role IDs.
  • Member, role, channel and permission information needed for server management and security.
  • Moderation events, audit-related events and security alerts.
  • Message content or message metadata when required by enabled logging, anti-spam, moderation, or backup features.
  • Command usage, configuration settings and bot error information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.
  • Backup and recovery information, if those features are enabled.

The exact information processed depends on the features enabled in a particular server. Do not assume that a feature stores data unless it is actually implemented and configured to do so.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide moderation and server management functions, detect suspicious activity, support anti-nuke protection, record authorized administrative actions, maintain configured backups, troubleshoot errors and improve reliability and security.

3. Message Logging and Backups

If message logging or backup features are enabled, relevant message content, identifiers, timestamps or channel information may be stored to provide those features. Server owners and administrators should configure logging responsibly and notify members where appropriate. JARVIS cannot recover information that was never recorded or that is no longer available.

4. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in the bot's configured database, files, backup system or hosting environment. Retention depends on the implemented features and server configuration. Data may remain until it is deleted, overwritten under a configured retention rule, or removed when the service is discontinued. The operator should configure appropriate retention and deletion procedures.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and by hosting or infrastructure providers required to operate the bot. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address security incidents, abuse or service integrity. Refer to Discord's own privacy policy for its data practices.

6. Access and Security

Access to stored information should be limited to authorized operators and systems. Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect stored data. No method of transmission, storage or automated security protection can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You can stop using JARVIS by asking a server administrator to remove it from the server. For questions or requests concerning data processed by the bot, contact its operator through the official support server or contact method provided by the operator. Requests may require verification and may be subject to legal or operational limitations.

8. Children's Privacy

JARVIS is intended to operate within Discord and is subject to Discord's age requirements. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. International Processing

Depending on the hosting provider and infrastructure used, information may be processed in locations different from the location of the Discord server or its members, subject to applicable law.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page. Material changes should be communicated where appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns, contact the JARVIS Ultimate operator through the official support server or the contact details published by the operator.
